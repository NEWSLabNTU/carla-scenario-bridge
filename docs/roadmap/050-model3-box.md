# Phase 050: The Model 3's Box in Every Scenario

Every scenario that names CARLA's `vehicle.tesla.model3` must declare the car's measured
box and axles, for the ego and for NPCs alike, and a lint must keep it that way. Until
this phase 128 of 132 such definitions declared a box 29 cm short and 31 cm too tall, its
rear 23 cm short of the car's, on a CARLA body whose rear axle sat 11.4 cm ahead of SSv2's
origin. So SSv2 measured distances, time to collision and contacts on a car that was not
the one Autoware drives or the one CARLA simulates. This phase runs before
[051](051-target-shape.md), whose catalog then starts from correct numbers. Design:
[design/vehicle-side.md](../design/vehicle-side.md), "What exists" and "SSv2 uses the same
car".

**Status**: complete, 2026-10-23. Results below; one correction to this plan's numbers
("Where it starts") came out of S2's measurement.

**Source**: user direction 2026-10-23: "I would fix the scenarios' wrong ego box", then "Go
ego and NPCs".
**Depends on**: nothing open. CARLA 0.9.16 for the measurement and the runs.
**Feeds**: [051](051-target-shape.md) (its catalog and lint start from these numbers); the gap campaign
(024-026), whose baselines are re-measured here once.

## Where it starts (2026-10-23)

Counted over every `.xosc` in `src/swb_examples/` and `scenarios/`:

| | definitions | wrong | right |
|---|---|---|---|
| ego (`vehicle.tesla.model3`) | 27 | 25 | 2 |
| NPCs and background AV (`vehicle.tesla.model3`) | 105 | 103 | 2 |
| any other vehicle named by a CARLA blueprint | 0 | | |
| the AWF YAML scenarios (`awf/*.yaml`, the ego placeholders) | 2 | 0 | 2 |

The wrong ones declare `Center (1.5, 0, 0.9)`, `Dimensions 4.5 x 2.1 x 1.8`, and axles
3.1 m apart, track 1.8 m, wheel diameter 0.6 m. The car is:

- 4.792 x 2.163 x 1.488 m (`carla_world/config/blueprint_boxes_0.9.16.yaml`);
- 3.005 m wheel base, 1.667 m track, 0.74 m wheel diameter (acb's `vehicle_info`, measured
  by `extract_vehicle_params.py`);
- so the box centre sits 1.532 m ahead of the rear axle and 0.744 m up.

**Corrected by S2 (2026-10-23).** The 1.532 above assumed the rear axle half a wheel base
behind CARLA's actor origin, as acb's `vehicle_info` overhangs do. `physics_control` puts it
at -1.386 and the front axle at +1.618, so the box centre is **1.415** m ahead of the rear
axle (0.029 + 1.386). With the old declaration the bridge put CARLA's actor origin 1.5 m
ahead of SSv2's origin, so the car's rear axle sat **11.4 cm** ahead of where SSv2 and
Autoware's localization took it to be; the old box's front was 6 cm short of the car's
(3.75 m from the rear axle against 3.811) and its rear 23 cm short (0.75 against 0.981).
The bullets below are the plan as drafted.

By file: bench 80 (generated by `gen_npc_benchmark.py`), gap 23, basic 11, demo incidents 6
(`gen_demo_incidents.py`), demo tour 1 (`gen_demo_tour.py`), surfaces 3, multi_av 2,
isaac 2, tutorial 1, and `scenarios/demo/town01_i6_cyclist_inside.xosc` 1.

Why it matters beyond the numbers:

- The bridge shifts every pose by the declared centre (`OriginOffset`). SSv2's ego origin is
  3.2 cm off Autoware's `base_link`.
- The box front is 3.75 m ahead of the rear axle in SSv2 and 3.93 m on the car, so every
  freespace distance to something ahead is 18 cm long.
- The gap oracle compares SSv2's declared box with CARLA's body, so it has been measuring
  this error as part of every gap.

## Design (as proposed; as built under "Results")

- **The measured table grows the axles.** `scripts/measure_blueprint_boxes.py` also
  records, from each vehicle's `physics_control`, the rear axle's x from the actor origin,
  the wheel base, the track and the wheel radius. `blueprint_boxes_0.9.16.yaml` is
  regenerated, and `blueprint_boxes.rs` reads and ignores the new keys.
- **One function writes the box.** A small Python module, `scripts/scenario_vehicle.py`,
  returns the OpenSCENARIO `BoundingBox` and `Axles` for a blueprint from the table:
  - centre x = the box centre's x minus the rear axle's x;
  - centre z = height / 2, since SSv2's entity origin is on the ground;
  - axles from the wheel geometry, rear axle at x 0.

  The three generators use it. `Performance` stays per scenario (051).
- **The lint** (`scripts/check_scenario_vehicles.py`, run by `just test`): every `<Vehicle>`
  whose `name` is a blueprint in the table must declare that box and those axles within
  2 cm. Failures name the file, entity, field, declared and measured values.
  - An entity may opt out only with an OpenSCENARIO `<Property name="swb.box_exempt"
    value="<reason>"/>`. The lint lists every exemption it allows.
  - None is expected: the gap scenarios' deliberate mismatches (`town01_truck_pass`'s 12 m
    truck) are declared under other names with `model3d`, not named
    `vehicle.tesla.model3`.

## Results (2026-10-23)

Run on newslab-118-server, this checkout's CARLA 0.9.16 (port 2010, Town01, Epic), the same
procedure before (`e9b3e60`'s parent, built fresh) and after (`e9b3e60`):
`scripts/run_050_set.sh <label>` runs the set with the bridge's gap traces on, and
`scripts/report_050.py` compares them (per ego pair: minimum box-to-box gap in SSv2's
geometry and CARLA's; contacts; gap oracle counts; frame budget). Logs:
`play_log/050/{before,after,after_truck,after_truck9}/`, report `play_log/050/report.md`.

### As built

- **The table** (`blueprint_boxes_0.9.16.yaml`, re-measured): boxes unchanged, and every one
  of the 41 vehicles gains `axles` (`front_x`, `rear_x` from the actor origin, rear `track`,
  `wheel_radius`). Model 3: front 1.618, rear -1.386 (wheel base 3.004), track 1.667,
  radius 0.37. The measure script tells world-centimetre wheel positions from actor-frame
  ones (CARLA reports the latter before the vehicle has been simulated).
- **`scripts/scenario_vehicle.py`**: Center (1.415, 0, 0.744), 4.792 x 2.163 x 1.488, front
  axle at 3.004, both at z 0.37, diameter 0.74, track 1.667. The incident, tour and bench
  generators use it and reproduce the committed files byte for byte; the other 21 files
  were rewritten with `--rewrite` (values only, layout kept). 132 vehicles in 31 `.xosc`;
  the two AWF YAML egos' centre 1.532 -> 1.415.
- **The world server lines the boxes up** (not in the plan; found by S2). It used the
  declared centre as the origin offset, so even a correct box left CARLA's car 2.9 cm off
  (CARLA's box centre is 0.029 ahead of the actor origin). Now a vehicle whose declared box
  equals its body's measured box (2 cm) has the table's box centre taken off the offset
  (`measured_vehicle_box_center`): SSv2's box and CARLA's coincide and the rear axle is on
  SSv2's origin. A declared size the body only fits keeps its centre as the offset, as
  before.
- **The lint** in `just test`: 132 checked, 0 exempt; a planted 4.5 m box fails with
  `src/swb_examples/scenarios/basic/town01_ego_drive.xosc: ego: BoundingBox/Dimensions
  length declared 4.5, measured 4.792`.
- **Fixtures**: only `swb_world_check`'s conformance spawn models the car and moved to the
  measured box. The rest that use 1.5/4.5 test arithmetic or the size match of a declared
  4.5 m car (still how a car declared by size gets its body) and stay.
- **`town01_truck_pass`'s hold** 8.5 -> 9.0 m (below).
- **`just two-av`** looked for the bridge under its pre-046 process name and refused to
  start; fixed (needed for S1).

### What moved, and why

- **Model 3s now agree to the centimetre.** Over the 43 ego pairs whose other entity is a
  Model 3, the median |SSv2 gap - CARLA gap| went 0.062 -> 0.030 m. Standing ones agree
  exactly: the tour's parked cars `pk_west_1..4`, `pk_bottom_1/2`, `i2_parked`, `i2_van`
  went from SSv2 3.2 cm long (e.g. 1.501 / 1.469) to equal (1.468 / 1.468), and
  `npc_autopilot` / `npc_route` 0.21-0.25 m -> 0.000. Moving ones keep the tracked NPCs'
  lag (`x_57w_1` 0.454 -> 0.334, `bg_av_1` 0.444 -> 0.152).
- **Other bodies** (walkers, props, size-matched NPCs): median 0.081 -> 0.112 m. What remains
  is each entity's own declared-vs-body difference; before, the ego's misplaced box partly
  cancelled it in some pairs.
- **Beside**: the parked cars and `barrier_0` are passed side by side, so their SSv2 gaps
  shrank by the half-width difference, 3.2 cm (2.1 -> 2.163 m wide).
- **Ahead**: the SSv2 box front is now the car's front. Before, SSv2's ego origin was read
  back 11.4 cm behind the real rear axle and the box front was 3.75 m ahead of it, so
  SSv2's front sat 17.5 cm behind the car's (actor origin + 2.25 against + 2.425). Stopped
  behind a car, SSv2 minus CARLA fell by exactly that: `i1_lead` 0.292 -> 0.117 m,
  `i4_broken` 0.292 -> 0.117. The 0.117 left is the lead's own declaration, the starter car
  box on a Model 3 body (its declared rear 0.117 m behind the body's). The physical (CARLA)
  stopping gaps are within run-to-run spread: `i1_lead` 4.82 -> 5.32 m standalone but
  5.36 -> 5.38 in the tour; `i4_broken` 5.40 -> 5.32.
- **`town01_truck_pass`**: the ego's CARLA body now sits on its rear axle, 0.114 m behind
  where the old offset put it, so CARLA's bodies meet at 7.41 m reference distance, not
  7.29. At the old 8.5 m hold 2 of 4 after-runs touched: the tracked truck body overshoots
  its SSv2 pose by up to 1.1 m when it brakes to hold from ~2.8 m/s (the before-run braked
  from 0.8). At 9.0 m, 4 of 4: G1 on the ego-truck pair (SSv2 overlap 2.08-2.28 m), no
  CARLA contact (clearance 0.85-1.16 m).
- **Frame budget** (bench, 20 NPCs): processing p50/p95 1.01/1.26 -> 0.98/1.21 ms, tick
  7.77/9.16 -> 7.77/8.87 ms. Unchanged.
